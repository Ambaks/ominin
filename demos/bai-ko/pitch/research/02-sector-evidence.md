## Things that contradict claims a pitch might naturally make

1. **"Les Français veulent commander sur QR code."** False for the general population: Lightspeed/Medallia (May 2024, n = 1 000) has >85 % preferring paper and 32 % disliking QR menus; Ipsos (US, 2025) has 58 % wanting paper back; NRA (US 2024) has only 48 % of full-service diners willing to order by QR. Acceptance is age-driven, and only that framing is honest.
2. **"Le QR code augmente le panier moyen de 10–20 %."** Only vendors say this. The one rigorous study finds ≈ +1 % per check; the gain is time (−10 % meal duration).
3. **"+40 % de pourboires."** sunday's own 2022 claim, unsourced. Its 2025 figure is +10 % (US). No independent data. Its YouGov poll also says 18 % never tip and 30 % rarely do.
4. **"Les étudiants vont dépenser plus."** 48 % of students have already gone without food for money reasons (Union étudiante, 2025), and since 04/05/2026 every student can eat at the Crous for 1 €. At the 10.90 € Bento, Baï-Kō competes on experience, not price.
5. **"Faire tourner les tables plus vite le soir."** Occitanie has France's longest average time at table (87 min, sunday 2024 data), and rushing the meal lowers satisfaction (Cornell 2007). Pitch faster ordering, reordering and paying, not a shorter meal. At lunch, speed is the argument.
6. **"Impossible de recruter des serveurs."** Overstated: difficulty fell (national 50 % → 42 % for servers; Occitanie 37 %, below average). It is still about 4 in 10, with 65 % of sector employees leaving within two years (AKTO).
7. **"La cuisine coréenne explose en France."** There is no independent national data. Korean food is absent from Deliveroo France's 2025 top 50 and from TheFork's 2024 rising cuisines, and the growth figures are Paris-only, old (2019) or company claims.
8. **"Uber Eats prend 30 %."** Uber's own rate card says 30 % (Standard) / 15 % pickup, but its footnote says the plans are for Japan only. The AN question says "pouvant atteindre 30 à 35 %" (an upper bound). Say "jusqu'à 30 % et plus en livraison, 15 % à emporter".
