# Le Victoria (3 place Castellane, Montpellier): carte collected from the web

Collected 2026-10-14.

## How this was collected (read first)

- Direct page fetching was impossible in this session. WebFetch failed on every host (DNS) and the
  egress proxy refused curl to every relevant host (privateaser, schlouk-map, deliveroo CDN,
  tripadvisor, instagram, restaurantguru, levictoriamontpellier.com, api.gouv.fr). Everything below
  comes from **search-engine snapshots of the pages** (WebSearch), not from fetching them.
- Where the snapshot quoted the page verbatim, the text is in "quotes". Everything else is the
  item name and price as the snapshot reported them, often paraphrased or translated. **Spelling,
  accents and capitalisation must be checked against the real page or with the owner.**
- No menu image could be downloaded. No date is visible on any of these sources, except the
  restaurantguru "owner menu", which is marked as about 4 years old (so around 2022).
- Nothing here is invented. Where an item is uncertain, it is flagged.

## E. Conflicts to resolve with the owner
1. Burger price: 11,90 € (Deliveroo) vs "from 12,90 €" or "12,90 €" (Privateaser wording) vs 13,90 € (restaurantguru, probably not theirs).
2. Pint price: 4,5 € [PA] vs 5,80 € for 50 cl [SM] vs 5,90 € [MGB]. The 4,5 € may be an old or happy-hour figure.
3. Large cocktails: 8 € for 50 cl (Deliveroo "XXL") vs Planteur 22 € / Mojito Classic 25 € / Coco Punch 18 € [LC]. These are probably different formats.
4. Poulpe vs seiche à la sétoise [RG].
5. Is there a current dine-in carte with entrées and plats (tartare, pièce du boucher, souris d'agneau), or has the venue moved to a pizza/burger/tapas format? Deliveroo shows the latter. The souris d'agneau is the only item that appears on both.
