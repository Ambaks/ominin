# Café Poulard: carte transcription, one section per source

Researched 2026-10-15. Sources are kept separate on purpose; nothing is merged. Spelling is copied as shown.
`[illisible]` = not readable, `[coupé]` = outside the photo frame.

## Bottom line first

- **No current carte with prices is published anywhere online.** The only current prices found are the coffee
  prices (source 1). Everything else with prices belongs to the **previous owners** (Les Frères Poulards
  bistro-rôtisserie, 2017 to ~2025), whose carte is obsolete.
- Ownership changed around winter 2025-26. Signals: Instagram @cafe_poulard starts on 2026-02-25; a Google review from
  ~April 2026 opens with "Changement de propriétaire"; PagesJaunes lists Les Frères Poulards as "Fermé
  définitivement"; the chef is now @renzoglt, and owner replies mention "Julien". The concept moved from bistro-rôtisserie
  (eggs, offal, roast meats) to a "comptoir méditerranéen" (tapas, fish crudo, plats du jour) that kept the rotisserie
  chicken.
- Takeaway: announced by the new owners on 2026-05-15 ("À emporter: Poulet rôti à la broche, sandwichs/burger/pita,
  plus d'infos bientôt"). No takeaway prices anywhere, and no Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat or TheFork listing,
  so there are no delivery-markup prices to compare.
- **What to do:** the current carte and prices have to come from the restaurant itself (a photo of the printed carte,
  the chalkboard and the takeaway list).
