# O'Crousti Poulet Original: network research for the head-office pitch

All sources were accessed on **2026-09-29** unless another date is given. "Site" means ocroustipouletoriginal.com.

Confidence tags used below:
- **[verified]**: read directly on a primary source (their site, a registry, INPI, or their own LinkedIn or TikTok).
- **[claim]**: a figure the company states about itself, which no one else has checked.
- **[inferred]**: my own deduction from other facts.
- **[unverified]**: seen only in a search-engine summary, or behind a page I could not open.

---

## 0. Key points to take into the pitch

1. **Restaurant count.** Say **"40 restaurants"** (their own current figure) or, more safely, "une quarantaine de restaurants". The website lists **38**. Their LinkedIn and TikTok say **40**. The two missing units are **Vichy** and **Paris 10e**. Details are in section 1.
2. **Growth pace.**
   - They say they had 15 restaurants in mid-January 2026 and 40 in late September 2026.
   - Their public target is **50 by December 2026**, with "20 nouvelles implantations en cours".
   - Every new opening could launch with QR ordering from day one.
3. **Revenue per restaurant.** The franchisor announces an **average revenue of €800,000 a year per shop** [claim]. The ~50 m² format is counter and takeaway only, open 7 days, usually from 11:00 or 11:30 to 23:00 (some until 01:00).
4. **Their pricing story conflicts with a 3 % commission.**
   - They sell franchises on a **fixed royalty of €2,000 a month, whatever the revenue**. They call it the "franchise du peuple".
   - A 3 % variable fee cuts against that story. Consider also offering a flat monthly price per restaurant, or position the 3 % against delivery-platform commissions.
   - They are also halal and refuse interest-bearing loans, so avoid any financing or credit angle.
5. **Tills and kiosks.** The till is a network standard: franchisee training covers "utilisation du logiciel de caisse", and the head-office field coordinator sets up the till tools before opening. **Which till system they use is not published anywhere.** I found no evidence of self-order kiosks, menu display screens, online ordering, an app or a loyalty programme.
6. **Head office imposes network-wide standards.**
   - They signed a PepsiCo drinks deal for all restaurants (June 2026).
   - Their Uber Eats integration is described as "géré pour vous".
   - They bought a beIN Sports World Cup ad (30 matches).

   These show head office can impose a tool across the whole network.
7. **Pain point to use: queues at the counter.** Their own marketing celebrates queues on the pavement. The site has a section titled "ÇA DÉFILE DÈS LE PREMIER JOUR", and the openings give away 300 free menus. QR ordering with numbered tickets addresses this directly.
8. **Best contact channel.**
   - Email **ocp.franchise@gmail.com**. It is the only email published anywhere; there is no corporate domain email.
   - Also use the contact form at https://ocroustipouletoriginal.com/contact and pick the subject "Presse & Partenariats".
   - In parallel, send a LinkedIn message to **Achraf Ben Taleb**, who is the public voice of development and purchasing.
9. **Other vendors are already approaching them.** Under the 28-Sept-2026 LinkedIn post, a commenter shown as "Jonathan Pariente Smart Touch" publicly offered to "vous accompagner" in rush periods.

**Conclusion.**
- **The most reliable current figure is 40, the company's own number as of 19–28 Sept 2026.** 38 of these appear on the website with addresses.
- The difference is two restaurants the company names on LinkedIn that are **not on the site**:
  1. **Vichy, 53 rue de Paris.** Confirmed in the company register: O'CP 03 SAS, SIREN 106881469, created 2026-06-12. Its Directeur Général is Abderrahim Safi.
  2. **Paris 10e, "quartier Château d'Eau".** Opened July–August 2026 according to Achraf Ben Taleb's 23-Aug post. I found no address, no page on the site and no registry match [unverified].
- The founder's belief of "38" matches the website exactly. The website simply lags behind openings.
- **Caveat: their own announcements do not add up exactly.**
  - 35 at the end of August plus 6 openings in September makes 41, not 40.
  - In late May they said both "25" (after 23 May) and "24th and 25th" (for 30 May).
  - In June they said both "26" (13 June) and "31" (21 June).

  Their figures are promotional and move quickly, so avoid quoting an exact number with false precision. "Une quarantaine" is always safe.
