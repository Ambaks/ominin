# O'Crousti Poulet x Ominin film: free music and SFX shortlist

Checked 2026-09-29. Nothing was downloaded. Every URL below was loaded (Pixabay pages in a browser, Mixkit and Freesound pages over HTTP 200). The track or sound title on each page matched.

**How to read the timestamps.** Structure marks (intro, drop, breakdown, end) come from the waveform each site draws on its page. Nobody listened to the tracks, so treat every mark as roughly ±2 s and confirm it by ear. Pixabay and Mixkit don't list BPM on their pages, so BPM is "not listed" throughout.

**Sources left out, and why:**
- **Uppbeat free tier:** it's 3 downloads, for "individual use on your own channels" only. Digital ads and company or client content need **Pro (€15.99/mo)** or **Business (€29.99/mo, excl. tax, yearly)**. The credit rule doesn't matter because this use isn't covered at all. See [uppbeat.io/pricing](https://uppbeat.io/pricing) and [commercial licenses](https://uppbeat.io/commercial-licenses).
- **YouTube Audio Library:** the official help page doesn't grant use outside YouTube. It only says Creative Commons tracks need a credit in the video description. The license is shown per track inside YouTube Studio. A pitch film will circulate outside YouTube, so this source is excluded ([help page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882)).
- **Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod):** the free option requires a credit; a paid "Standard License" removes it. The sound is dated and overused, so it doesn't meet the bar ([licenses](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/)).
- **Free Music Archive / ccMixter:** licenses vary per track and many are NC. The CC0 artists I checked (HoliznaCC0: mostly lo-fi; Loyalty Freak Music) don't reach the quality bar. I didn't search ccMixter in depth.
- **AlexGuz (Pixabay):** his page text says YouTube use needs a separate paid license. That conflicts with the Pixabay license and signals claim hassle, so I avoided him. The same applies to the very popular but overused moodmode track ("No Copyright Music", 389k downloads).

## 5. Caveats
- **Content ID:** M1, M3, M4 and M7 are CID-registered. If the film goes on YouTube, a claim may appear. Clear it with the license certificate Pixabay offers at download, or via the claim guide linked above. Sending the file directly, LinkedIn and the pitch meeting itself are unaffected. M2 and M5 aren't registered.
- **Mixkit music (M6):** fine online. It is **not** licensed for TV or radio broadcast.
- **Freesound:** downloading requires a free account. A few of these sounds are mirrored on Pixabay under "freesound_community", which avoids the account.
- **Keep the proof:** for each asset, save the page URL, the file name and the license link. Download Pixabay's certificate for CID tracks.
- **Timestamps** come from the waveforms, not from listening. Confirm the drops by ear before locking the edit.
