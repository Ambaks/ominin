## 9. Reviews — themes from extracts (thin evidence)
- Praise: friendly staff, low prices / happy hour, terrace in the sun, mojitos.
- Complaints: slow service, average cocktails. The Tripadvisor score (2.3) is much lower than the Google-mirror score (3.9).
- Signature items: mojitos (Facebook), the two "Signature" cocktails (Le N'Fire, El Marez), XXL cocktails, and per Privateaser
  a paella "façon Victoria" and a homemade mango tiramisu (unverified).
