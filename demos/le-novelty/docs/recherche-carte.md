# Le Novelty — carte collected from the web (2026-10-14)

## How this was collected (read first)

- Every direct fetch was blocked in this session: the egress proxy refused all
  hosts with a 403 (lenovelty-montpellier.fr, privateaser, schlouk-map,
  data.gouv APIs, Instagram, Facebook, Google, Tripadvisor, restaurantguru,
  web.archive.org…), and WebFetch could not resolve DNS. **No page was opened
  directly and no image could be downloaded.**
- Everything below comes from search-engine extracts of the pages (WebSearch).
  Prices and names were cross-checked over 2–4 independent queries where
  possible. Where extracts disagreed, both readings are listed. Ingredient lists
  marked "verbatim" were quoted exactly in the extract. The others are the
  extract's wording, so treat their order and spelling as approximate.
- Main source: **S1 = https://lenovelty-montpellier.fr/** (the venue's own site,
  homepage section "Notre Carte", tabs: Cocktails · Bières · Softs & Cafés ·
  Restauration). **Date not visible.** The site gives the address 10 rue Saint-Guilhem,
  the phone 04 34 35 36 28 and the Instagram handle @le_novelty_bar_montpellier, so it is the current venue.
  The "Restauration" tab is a placeholder ("La suite arrive bientôt"), which suggests the site
  is recent and still being built.
- The site does not say whether the prices are normal or happy-hour prices. The prices
  look like normal prices, because HH is advertised separately as "prix réduits".
- Other sources: S2 Privateaser, S3 Schlouk Map, S4 MisterGoodBeer (see the end of this file).

Banner on S1: **"Happy Hours — Tous les jours de 17h à 23h, profitez de nos bières, cocktails et shots à prix réduits."** (verbatim)
