# Rosemarie (Montpellier) - carte transcription

Research date: 2026-10-24. No menu from the owner. The only primary carte evidence found is five user-uploaded photos of the physical menu on Restaurant Guru. Photos are undated; dating below is inferred from Instagram posts and is flagged as inference.

Source page: https://restaurantguru.com/Rosemary-Montpellier/menu (fetched with curl; image host https://menu02.restaurantguru.com/m3/). Local copies in `img/menu/`.

Prices are in euros, "prix net, service compris" (printed on the older cream menu). Wording is kept as printed, including the printed typos (e.g. "Éffiloché", "boeuf", "chataignes", "Fettucine", "fânes").
