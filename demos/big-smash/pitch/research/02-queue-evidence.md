# Big Smash: what the public says about waiting, queues, rush hours and order accuracy

Research for the Ominin pitch to Big Smash head office (QR ordering from the table or the queue, numbered live tickets, "prête" notification on the customer's phone). Everything was checked on **2026-10-03** from a logged-out browser located in France (Google placed it in Montpellier). All counts are exact counts over the samples described below. None of them are extrapolated.

**Framing advice.** The data does not support "Big Smash customers complain about waiting". The network is rated 4.67★, and in Google's top sample speed praise (22%) is almost three times as common as wait complaints (8%). What the data does support:

1. **Order taking is already digital.** The brand's FAQ mentions kiosks in its restaurants, and click & collect works in all of them. The pain points come after the order:
   - the order sits ready on the pass while nobody announces it (15 min at Montpellier, about 15 min at Échirolles, 20 min at Fenouillet);
   - the bag goes to the wrong customer (Croix de Pierre, and head office agreed);
   - one person handles both the till and the hand-off (Matabiau);
   - customers who order at the kiosk still queue a second time to pay at the counter (Montpellier, Fenouillet).
2. **The long waits cluster in new restaurants**, which is where Big Smash is growing (target: 10 openings a year). In 2026 the reviews describe 20–30 min "pour deux burgers", even at quiet hours, plus understaffing ("manque de personnel", 4 reviews).
3. **Speed is what customers value.** The brand sells itself as a "service rapide", and the 5★ reviews keep saying "rapide".

So the pitch is: **make "where is my order" visible on the customer's phone.**
- A numbered live ticket and a "prête" push let the customer collect the moment the order is ready.
- Checking the number at the counter stops wrong bags.
- Paying on the phone removes the second queue at the counter.
- Head office gets per-restaurant preparation times, which shows which new openings are struggling.

**Do not claim that QR ordering fixes kitchen speed**: most of the long waits happen after the order is placed.

Reviews cannot measure people who walk away. To quantify that, ask head office for kiosk/POS order timestamps versus hand-off times per restaurant, or observe a new restaurant (Montaudran or Fenouillet) at the Friday 19h–21h rush.

## 1. Method and limits (read before quoting any number)

- **Location list.** bigsmash.fr lists 10 restaurants (page read 2026-10-03):
  - Toulouse Bayard (= Matabiau), Toulouse Croix de Pierre, Montpellier (121 av. de Palavas), Échirolles, Muret, Fenouillet (Saint-Alban), Lyon 2e, Toulouse Montaudran, Blagnac, Paris 18e.
  - Announced: Nîmes, Le Havre, "Toulouse 4", Carcassonne.
  - The brand's Instagram bio (seen only as a Google snippet) claims 11 restaurants. Older TikTok captions also list Colomiers (2024) and Grenoble, 11 rue de Belgrade (early 2026). Neither is on the site or on Google Maps today, so both are excluded.
- **Tangier is excluded.** The franchise page claims a 2024 opening there. Google Maps has no "Big Smash Tanger" listing, only two "New School Tacos Tanger" listings (4.5★/922 and 4.0★/529), and nothing shows Big Smash operates inside them. TripAdvisor has no Big Smash in Tangier either.
- **Google Maps, logged out (Sample A, n = 50).** For each restaurant, by its cid link:
  - The view shows the rating, the review count, the exact 1–5★ breakdown, Google's top 10 review topics, and **only the 5 "most relevant" reviews**.
  - Sorting, filtering by topic and "Plus d'avis" all ask you to sign in. They were not used.
  - The sample is Google's relevance ranking, which favours long, detailed reviews. It is **not random and not the newest**. It is the only basis in this file for percentages.
- **Google Search review page, logged out (Sample B).**
  - The "Afficher tous les avis" link in the Google Search knowledge panel opens a reviews page with a working **"Rechercher dans les avis"** box, without signing in.
  - Keywords searched at each restaurant: attente, minutes, lent, rapide, borne, bornes, numéro, écran, click, erreur, oublié (and oubli / oublient / manquait / manquantes where relevant), manque, monde, affluence, comptoir, prête / prêts, prénom, inauguration, file, rush, personnel.
  - Limits, all observed directly:
    - **At most 20 matches are displayed per query.** "rapide" hit that cap at 6 of 10 restaurants.
    - **The search is not exhaustive.** At Croix de Pierre, a review saying "beaucoup d'attente" came up under "monde" but not under "attente".
    - **It matches exact words.** "oubli" does not find "oublié", and "borne" does not find "bornes".
    - Results show a snippet around the keyword. Full text was opened for 2 reviews only. Google's dialog shows them auto-translated into English, so French wording is only available from the snippets.
  - **Use Sample B for themes and "at least N" statements only, never for percentages.** It is keyword-targeted, so it over-represents operational complaints.
- **Counting rules for Sample B.**
  - A "wait complaint" (W−) is a review that criticises a wait or slowness, including mild ones ("un peu d'attente").
  - Reviews that accept a wait because of a crowd ("victime de leur succès", "vu le monde") count as neutral, not as complaints.
  - Duplicates found under several keywords count once.
  - One review whose snippet was truncated before any content was excluded.
  - Durations are taken as stated, with the lower bound for ranges ("25 à 30" counts as 25).
  - Dates are approximate: the "Visité en" month when shown, otherwise "il y a X" counted back from 2026-10-03.
- **Co-branded stores.** Fenouillet and Lyon are "New School Tacos X Big Smash" store-in-store units, so many of their complaints are about the tacos. They share the counter, the kiosks and the kitchen, so the operational complaints still apply. They are flagged with ‡ throughout.
- **Signs of solicited 5★ reviews** (they inflate ratings, not complaint counts):
  - clusters of very short 5★ "service rapide" reviews in opening weeks (Fenouillet Feb–Mar 2026, Lyon May 2026, Montaudran Jun 2026);
  - Paris 18 has 213 reviews and none at 1–2★;
  - a 3★ Montaudran reviewer says many 5★ reviews have no photos and praise a "personnel à l'écoute" even though ordering is done at kiosks.
- **Delivery ratings.**
  - Uber Eats: rating and count from each store page's structured data, read in the browser. No review text without an address.
  - Deliveroo: rating and count from the store page header. Opening the reviews panel asks for a delivery address, which was not entered.
- **Blocked or thin sources**:
  - Restaurant Guru: main pages show only the latest 3 reviews; the `/reviews` pages return HTTP 503.
  - TripAdvisor: only "Big Smash Montaudran" (0 reviews), plus an unrelated-looking "Big smash burger" in Lyon (0 reviews).
  - Glassdoor: served a bot check, which was not bypassed.
  - Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn: login walls, so only Google snippets of brand posts were read.
  - TikTok: the profile's video grid would not load ("Something went wrong"). Captions of 4 brand videos were read through TikTok's public oEmbed.
  - restaurants-de-france.fr: its review section is now empty. A Google summary of an older version (Montpellier: "393 comments", an order "15 minutes on the counter") could not be verified and is not used.
  - Rankeat: its text about "service rapide même aux heures de pointe" is labelled as AI-generated by the site, so it is not used.
  - The WebSearch quota ran out late in the session. The last searches were done in the browser's Google.
- **Quoting policy** (same as for the O'Crousti report): our copyright rules allow one short verbatim quote per deliverable.
  - **Only E1 in §4 is verbatim.** All other review evidence is paraphrased and labelled as paraphrase, with the reviewer's own keywords in «guillemets» (1–4 word tags, not sentences).
  - To put exact wording on a slide, open the listing (link, rating and approximate date given) and copy it from the source. Never attribute it to a reviewer by name.
- **Privacy:** no reviewer names, and no names of staff mentioned in reviews, are recorded anywhere in this file.

## 3. What the reviews say, by theme

### 3a. Sample A: Google's 5 "most relevant" reviews per restaurant (n = 50)

- **Speed is praised more often than waiting is criticised.** 11 reviews at 7 restaurants call the service «rapide», «efficace» or say there was «peu d'attente».
  - Two of them name the rush explicitly: Muret is fast «même lorsqu'il y a du monde», and a Blagnac reviewer recommends going «heures non affluentes».
- **4 wait complaints, 3 of them at 1★**:
  - Croix de Pierre: 27 min while alone in the restaurant at 19h30, with a wrong order and the order called by first name.
  - Croix de Pierre: 1 h for 2 click & collect menus, with a burger missing.
  - Fenouillet ‡: about 15 min as the only customer.
  - Muret: a 3★ review of the inauguration, «très long» to get going and «pagaille».
- **4 wrong or incomplete orders**, at Croix de Pierre (2) and Fenouillet ‡ (2).
- **How people ordered.**
  - 2 reviews mention the kiosk (Échirolles: lots of choice «sur les bornes»; Montaudran: orders are «principalement sur borne»).
  - 2 mention a staff member taking or helping with the order (Matabiau, Muret).
- **Half of the 6 one-star reviews in the sample are about waiting** (Croix de Pierre ×2, Fenouillet ‡). The other 3 are about food (a burger left too long in the chute, little meat, tacos quality).

**5. Ordering method: kiosks confirmed, with friction (27 reviews mention them).**
- **The brand's FAQ** says modifications are possible «sur nos bornes en restaurant», on click & collect or on delivery apps.
- **What reviews describe**:
  - all orders go through kiosks (Matabiau 2022, Montaudran 2026);
  - kiosk bugs (Matabiau, Lyon ‡);
  - kiosk out of order (Matabiau);
  - three quarters of the menu missing on the kiosk (Blagnac);
  - kiosk then pay at the counter (Montpellier: waited ≥10 min to pay; Fenouillet ‡: 45 min after paying by ANCV voucher at the counter);
  - one customer refused the kiosk, ordered with staff, and got no drinks (Fenouillet ‡);
  - staff thanked for helping at the kiosk (3: Matabiau, Fenouillet ‡ ×2);
  - menus displayed above the kiosks (Matabiau).

**6. How orders are called: number and screen, or first name and voice.**
- **Number and screen:**
  - Matabiau 2022: the order's progress can be followed «sur une grande écran».
  - Matabiau 2026: asked to repeat the «numéro de commande», the employee pointed at «l'écran».
  - Croix de Pierre 2026: the customer asked why staff did not check the ticket and order number before handing out a bag.
- **First name or voice:**
  - Croix de Pierre 2026: hears «Commande d'[prénom]».
  - Matabiau 2024: the only complaint was the voice announcing each time that an order is «prête».
  - Montpellier 2024: likes the «commande avec les noms».

**7. Click & collect (6 reviews: 4 negative, 2 positive).**
- Negative:
  - 1 h for 2 menus, with a burger missing (Croix de Pierre, Sep 2025);
  - slot times «pas respectés» (Matabiau, Feb 2026);
  - waited 15 min on site, order not 100% right, «dépassé par leur succès» (Fenouillet ‡, May 2026);
  - first order, burger without its cheddar (Croix de Pierre).
- Positive:
  - collected before the slot time (Matabiau, Jul 2025);
  - «très rapide» (Fenouillet ‡, Mar 2026).

**8. Staffing named as the cause (6 reviews).**
- «manque de personnel» at Matabiau 2023 and Blagnac 2026;
- «manque clairement de personnel» at Fenouillet ‡, Sep 2026;
- a cashier alone «avec cette affluence» at Fenouillet ‡;
- a customer "ready to put on the apron and go behind the counter to help" (Muret);
- «manque de formation» in a just-opened restaurant (Montaudran).

**9. Recovery that works (worth citing to head office).**
- Muret: the kitchen and counter apologised in person for about a 25 min wait and offered a drink, and the review is 4★.
- Muret: a forgotten item was replaced by an ice cream, and the review is 5★.

**Popular times (thin).** Only Matabiau's Google panel was read, and only for Saturday. At the time of the check (Saturday evening) it said «Plus fréquenté que d'habitude», with an average stay «de 15 min à 1,5 h». The other days could not be loaded, so no peak-hour claim is made from it.

**Operations signals from reviews** (details in §3b):
- **Thin staffing and the rush:**
  - a cashier alone at the rush (Fenouillet ‡);
  - «manque de personnel» (Matabiau, Blagnac, Fenouillet ‡);
  - a customer tempted to help behind the counter (Muret);
  - training gaps in a new restaurant (Montaudran).
- **The till and the hand-off compete for the same person:**
  - the server takes payments while a ready order waits (Matabiau);
  - nobody at the counter while the order sits on the pass (Montpellier);
  - orders not announced (Échirolles).
- **Order-number discipline:** a bag handed to the wrong customer, and the Big Smash team agreeing that number checks are relevant (Croix de Pierre).
- **Kiosk dependence:** kiosk bugs and missing items (Matabiau, Lyon ‡, Blagnac), and a kiosk out of order (Matabiau).

- Kiosks in every restaurant (brand FAQ).
