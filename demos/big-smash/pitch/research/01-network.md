# BIG SMASH: network research for the head-office pitch

All sources were accessed on **2026-10-03** unless another date is given. "Site" means bigsmash.fr (Webflow; every page carries the stamp "Last Published: Mon Jul 06 2026"). "Online store" means shop.bigsmash.fr, their click & collect site.

Confidence tags used below:
- **[verified]**: read directly on a primary source (their site, their online store, a registry, their own LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook or Instagram page).
- **[claim]**: a figure the company states about itself, which no one else has checked.
- **[inferred]**: my own deduction from other facts.
- **[unverified]**: seen only in a search-engine summary, or behind a page I could not open.

Raw snapshots saved in `scratchpad/assets/docs/`: `site-home-2026-10-03.html`, `site-franchise-2026-10-03.html`, `mentions-legales-2026-10-03.html`, `site-la-carte-2026-10-03.html`, `site-equipe-2026-10-03.html`, `shop-points-de-vente-2026-10-03.html` (the restaurant list behind the online store; `/restaurants` returns 404 on the site), `commandes-delicity-montpellier-2026-10-03.html`, `linkedin-company-2026-10-03.html`.

## 0. Key points to take into the pitch

1. **Restaurant count. Say "10 restaurants" or "une dizaine de restaurants en France".**
   - The site and the live online store list the same 10 French restaurants. Two of them are "store-in-store" units shared with New School Tacos (Fenouillet and Lyon).
   - The franchise page still says "7 restaurants ouverts (mai 2025)".
   - Six more are announced: Nîmes, Le Havre, "Toulouse 4", Carcassonne, Vienne, and Bruay-la-Buissière (a store-in-store with a drive).
   - **Do not cite Tangier.** The franchise page claims a 2024 opening there, but it is on no current list and I found no trace of it. Details are in section 1.
2. **Who decides: Big Smash now belongs to the NSup group (New School Up), not to one founder.**
   - The franchisor company is MASTER BS, created 29/01/2025. NEW SCHOOL UP is its Président, and NEW SCHOOL UP is itself controlled by NST INDUSTRY (Selim Jaouadi and Anthony Mazzone).
   - SNS MK CONCEPT is its Directeur Général. That company belongs to Rachid Kaïd and Fethi Mabrouka.
   - The press names **Kaïd and Mabrouka as the founders of Big Smash** (2019, a 9 m² unit in Colomiers).
   - The site presents **Selim Jaouadi** in "Le mot du fondateur". His LinkedIn says "Co-fondateur du groupe NSup — New School Tacos, Big Smash, Chickeez".
   - **Safe wording:** call Selim "co-fondateur du groupe NSup", not "le fondateur de Big Smash", in case Kaïd or Mabrouka are in the room.
3. **Their stack is already digital, so this is not a first digital project for them.**
   - The FAQ mentions "nos bornes en restaurant" [claim].
   - Click & collect runs in every restaurant on shop.bigsmash.fr. It is a white-label Teréza store in the 6XPos ecosystem, with card payment through Stripe and loyalty switched on [verified].
   - A loyalty card runs on Avomark's "MaCarte" (bigsmash.macarte.club) [verified].
   - Delivery runs through Uber Eats and Deliveroo. The Montpellier franchisee orders through Delicity instead (commandes.bigsmash.fr) [verified].
   - **Pitch angle: QR ordering as "la borne dans la poche".** It extends the kiosks and the click & collect they already have to the table and the queue. It is not "passer au digital".
4. **Competitive risk: both of their current vendors already sell QR ordering at the table.**
   - 6XPos sells "QR Code à table", along with kiosks, KDS and a customer call screen.
   - Teréza sells "QR Code de commande à table" in its premium plan.
   - None of the 10 stores has a dine-in or table service switched on today (only "Click & Collect") [verified].
   - Ominin must say why it beats "switch on the module we already pay for": the live numbered ticket up to "prête", printing straight to the kitchen, the price, and the multi-brand basket (point 5).
5. **Store-in-store is the hook nobody else has a story for.**
   - Fenouillet (210 m²) and Lyon already sell Big Smash and New School under one roof. Bruay-la-Buissière (with a drive) opens at the end of 2026.
   - Their own words are "Mutualiser nos stocks et nos ressources" and "Optimiser nos opérations".
   - **The idea to pitch:** one QR code that orders tacos and burgers in a single basket, one payment and one numbered ticket.
6. **Economics fit a commission model.**
   - Franchisees already pay percentage fees: **5 % "redevance métier" plus 2 % marketing**, on top of a €25k entry fee [claim].
   - The franchisor announces **€1.1M average revenue at 2 years**, which is about **€3k a day** [claim].
   - So a variable fee does not clash with their story, unlike a fixed-royalty chain.
7. **The group is about 55 restaurants, so Big Smash is the door to NSup.**
   - New School Tacos has about 38–40 units (38 on Toute la Franchise; 35 points of sale and 20 openings targeted for 2025). Chickeez has 3 open and 6 signed (Selim Jaouadi, LinkedIn).
   - All the brands share one development team and the same web agency (Elypse) and ordering platform (Teréza / 6XPos).
   - At New School, delivery is only **about 10 % of sales**, so most volume happens in the restaurant (Zepros, Feb 2025) [claim].
8. **Mirror their voice.** They use "tu" with customers and a street-culture tone.
   - "1,2,3 ... SMASH !"
   - "fast food premium"
   - "fait maison"
   - "un service rapide centré sur la satisfaction client"
   - "Taste from States"
   - "THE PLACE TO BEAT !"
   - "ça va smasher"
   - "#TeamSmash / #TeamChicken"
   - Franchise pitch: "simple à exploiter", "exécution rapide en cuisine", "process clairs et rodés"
9. **Drinks: Coca-Cola system.** The menu lists Coca-Cola and Fuze Tea Pêche, plus Capri-Sun, Badoit, Evian, Volvic and Red Bull. **Never show a Pepsi product.** New School Tacos is also on Coca-Cola (Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fuze Tea).
10. **Contact.**
    - No head-office phone number is published.
    - The official email is **contact@bigsmash.fr** (it also serves as the data-protection address). Customer service is **sav@bigsmash.fr**.
    - There is a multi-step franchise form on /franchise.
    - Since the team knows the boss, a direct intro beats any form.
    - NSup's head office is at 7 rue Isabelle Eberhardt, 31200 Toulouse. Big Smash's teams moved into these shared offices in July 2026.
    - Next public event: the **Salon de la Street Food, 7–8 Nov 2026, Villepinte**. They had a joint New School × Big Smash stand at the 2025 edition.

## 8. Gaps and cautions

- **Founder narrative.** The press (Snacking) credits Rachid Kaïd and Fethi Mabrouka as founders. The site's "mot du fondateur" features Selim Jaouadi. Registry: Jaouadi and Mazzone's holding has presided SMASH N SHAKE since 2021, and the "acquisition" (NSup / MASTER BS) dates from early 2025. Use "co-fondateurs" and "groupe NSup" wording.
- **Tangier and Grenoble.** Both are claimed in older material and appear in no current list. Do not cite them.
- **Stale company figures.** "7 restaurants (mai 2025)" is still on the franchise page. The site's © says 2025, and the last publish was 06/07/2026. The store locator subdomain restaurants.bigsmash.fr is offline (TLS failure).
- **Till and kiosk brand not confirmed.** 6XPos is inferred from the store's domain and CDN. Zelty support also exists in the platform. Ask head office: till brand, kiosk brand, kitchen printers (brand and model), and whether KDS screens are in use.
- **Incumbent vendors already offer QR at the table** (6XPos "QR Code à table", Teréza "QR Code de commande à table"). Expect "we can switch that on with our current supplier". Prepare the differentiation.
- **Franchise economics are claims.** They come from Kapp Retail and the franchisor (€25k / 5 % / 2 % / €80k / €300k / €1.1M). No independent check exists. The average ticket is not public.
- **Opening dates are often inferred.** They rest on registry dates, a mall page without a year, or LinkedIn inauguration posts. Paris 18 status is "listed and store page created in Sept 2026". I did not confirm when it actually opened.
- **Team turnover.** Yves Gaspard's LinkedIn now reads "Freelance" [unverified]. Benjamin Schmit's exact current title is unverified.
- **The online store's public page source exposes internal configuration fields.** I read only what the public page sends to every visitor. **Do not mention any of these fields in the pitch**, as it would look like probing their systems.
- **Name confusion.** Many unrelated "Big Smash", "Smash" and "Big M" businesses exist (see §1.2).
- **Blocked sources.** Uber Eats and Deliveroo returned 403, so delivery prices, markups and the exact delivery coverage per restaurant are unverified. Instagram bios could not be read (counts only). Indeed and Restaurant Guru also returned 403.
- **Press coverage I could not find.** La Dépêche, Actu.fr, France 3, Néo-Restauration and RestoNews had nothing on Big Smash in the searches I ran. Snacking's 19/09/2025 article is behind a subscriber wall, so only its summary was read.
