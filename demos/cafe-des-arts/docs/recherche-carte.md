# Café des Arts (3 rue Saint-Guilhem, Montpellier): carte collected from the web

Collected 2026-10-14. **How it was collected:** WebFetch could not resolve any host and the
curl proxy refused every non-registry domain (403), so no page was opened directly.
Everything below comes from **search-engine extractions** of the pages listed. A string
in "quotes" was returned verbatim from the page text, typos included. Unquoted
descriptions are paraphrased by the search tool. Treat every price as **to be confirmed
with the owner**.

No source shows a date on its prices. **No menu image could be downloaded** because
every image host was blocked.

## Coverage verdict

- About **37 priced items** (formules 9 incl. 4 brasserie variants, entrées/partage 4,
  salades 5, bruschettas 3, burgers 2, viandes 2, poissons 3, pizzas 2, desserts/glaces 7),
  plus about 8 named dishes without prices. **Drinks are essentially missing:** only the
  1 € coffee and an unverified pint price.
- Nearly all priced items come from the **old official site**. Its formules (10 €, 12.90 €,
  15.50 €, 19.90 €) do not match the current site, which offers plats du jour from 9,90 €.
  This is consistent with a carte that is at least one revision old, probably several
  years old (C.K.L changed president in May 2023 and the group's new site is
  cafedesarts34.fr).
- Good enough to build a **credible demo** with a "prix indicatifs, à confirmer" mindset.
  Not good enough to go live without the owner's current paper carte and drinks list.
