# Toast' Tea (Montpellier) — carte transcription

Research date: 2026-10-16. Everything below was read off menu photos I downloaded myself (paths in the last section).
Spelling, capitalisation and punctuation follow the print, including quirks. `[?]` marks an uncertain reading,
`[…]` marks text hidden or cut off in the photo. No price or dish was invented. Where a section is missing,
it says so.
